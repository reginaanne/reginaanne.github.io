About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Kreiner Lab at The University of Chicago, where I study the evolution of domesticated and weedy species in the Amarnathus genus.

I completed my PhD in Populaion Biology advised by Dr. Jeffrey Ross-Ibarra at UC Davis in 2026. Before that, I completed a BA in Biology with minors in Anthropology and Archaeological Science at the University of Pennsylvania.

Research

My work focuses on how plants evolve in human-modified landscapes. I am particularly interested in the origin and evolution of domesticated plant species.
